Fact 1: The amazing octopus.

Octupuces have three hearts, nine brains and blue blood. Two hearts pump blood to the gills, while the third pumps to the rest of the body.

How smart are the octupuces?

Octupuces can open jar, solve puzzels, and even recognise human faces. Scientists consider them one of the most intelligent invertebrates on the Earth.

Fact 2: Lighting Strikes

Lighting strikes the Earth about 100 times every single second. That is roughly 8 million lighting strikes per day!

How hot is lightning?

A single bolt of lighting is around 30,000 Kelvin - five times hotter than the surface of the Sun.

Can lighting strike twice

Yes! Lighting absolutely can and does strike the same place more than once, especially tall structures like tower and trees.

Fact 2: The deep ocean

More than 80% of the worlds ocean remain unexplored. Scientist belive there are millions of species living in the deep sea that we have nevere discoverd.

How deep does the ocean go?

The deepest point on the Earth is the MAriana Trench, which reaches s depth of about 11 kilometers - deep enough to fit Mount Everest inside with a room to spare

Fun comparison

If you place Mount Everst at the bottom of the Mariana Trench, its peak would still be more than 2 kilmeters underwater.
